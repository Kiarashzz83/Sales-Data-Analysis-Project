This project analyzes sales data to extract business insights about 
customers, products, cities, and time trends.

#Key Metrics#
Total Revenue: 13500.0
Best Customer: Ali
Best Product: phone
Top city: Berlin


#Key Insights#
- A small group of customers generates a large portion of total revenue.
- Some products generate high revenue despite low quantity sold.
- Sales are concentrated in a few key cities.
- Revenue shows fluctuations over time indicating possible seasonality.

#Business Impact#
The business is highly dependent on a small number of customers and products, which 
represents both opportunity and risk.

#Recommendation#
Focus on retaining high-value customers, diversifying product performance, and 
expanding in underperforming cities.
